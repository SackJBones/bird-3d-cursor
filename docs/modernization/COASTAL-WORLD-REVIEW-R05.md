# Coastal world independent design review: R05

Independent critic review, 2026-09-28. **Final for the frozen R05 visual set and
the validation records below.** This report does not replace the
[R01–R04 review](COASTAL-WORLD-REVIEW.md) or change its scores. Implementation
and critique are performed separately. The tracking lab is outside this review.

## Intent and standard

The critic re-read the complete [concept conversation and four visual
references](../../../bird-3d-cursor-projects/Reference/WorldDesign20260927/README.md)
and the [maintained design brief](WORLD-DESIGN.md). Their spatial intent takes
precedence over copying individual image details: a quiet, generous white
arrival cavern; a plain rear wall; Bird and a clean traversable circular opening
as the focal point; inviting side rooms; an architectural first reveal before
the coast opens up; substantial flowing support and inhabited white masses;
cozy cliff-side destinations and sparse higher lookouts; a lower water edge and
one distant bounded island. The omitted generated floor plan is not approved.

The architecture should read as bright neutral white and mostly matte. Better
form definition must come from coherent geometry and economical lighting, not
beige materials, gloss or decorative density. Calm central volumes do not need
another conspicuous lounge. Their inhabited character can come from plausible
floor thickness, overhangs, openings and an occupied edge. Editability remains
essential.

The same rubric applies as before: 5 means recognizable intent with major
unresolved weaknesses; 7 means coherent and usable with material shortcomings;
8 means strong fit without major route or comfort defects in the reviewed
evidence; 9 requires unusually convincing work and runtime support. Navigation
includes legibility, not just connectivity. Overall quality is not an arithmetic
average. An early stage and a proposed target do not earn favorable scores.

## First-pass visual review

The critic inspected all twenty images in the local, ignored
[R05-first-pass snapshot](../../../bird-3d-cursor-projects/Validation/CoastalWorld/R05-first-pass),
plus its routes, geometry inventory and editability record. This is interim
evidence, not yet the durable final review snapshot. The roofs-hidden plan is
diagnostic; it is not a visible runtime view.

### Improvements supported by the images

- The large flat beams across arrival-wing circles are gone. Return views
  `13`/`14` and new lateral views `18`/`19` show short circular passages with
  coherent proportions and clear movement back into the cavern. Floor
  transitions no longer carry the conspicuous projecting strips.
- `11-cliff-room-looking-out` has one primary opening instead of competing
  portal layers. The cleaner floor and reduced fill hotspot make that room
  easier to read, although the stair still occupies much of its outer view.
- The tide room has a distinct L-shaped seating arrangement, a side art focus
  and a short turning approach. `16-tide-room-seated-coast` removes the staircase
  from the primary seated outlook. `17-tide-room-approach` shows an inviting
  interior destination rather than a route crossing the doorway.
- The central arrival composition, plain rear wall, clear gallery sculpture
  and separate social choices remain intact. These are retained strengths,
  rather than new R05 achievements.

### Issues reported after the first pass

The following records the feedback given before the final local corrections.
The final assessment below distinguishes resolved items from remaining work.

1. **Finish the tide-room reward.** In `16`, a large near headland still fills
   most of the circle; calm water occupies only a narrow lower/right area. The
   change fixes the stair obstruction but only partly achieves a peaceful
   coastal outlook. Shift or turn the actual room/seated relationship so open
   water becomes the principal view and the cliff acts as a framing edge.
   Recheck the entry and approach alongside the seated view; moving only the
   saved camera would not solve the room.
2. **Inspect the curved joins in motion.** `18` and `19` contain thin bright or
   dotted lines around the throat junctions, and `18` has a tiny cyan slit high
   in the cavern. Static evidence does not establish whether every line is a
   geometric gap, a seam or depth interference. These are substantially smaller
   than the removed beams, but should be inspected from lateral positions and
   fixed before declaring enclosure continuity complete.
3. **Make the main volume architectural.** `07-exterior` still reads as two
   stacked observation disks and appended barrel rooms; `02-threshold` is
   dominated by a broad, flat disk underside. The new `20-upper-floor` confirms
   an exposed circulation deck with railings and stairs rather than convincing
   inhabited form. A restrained asymmetrical shoulder or occupied curved edge
   growing into the support is still the highest-impact whole-world change.
   Keep the highest lookout sparse and preserve Bird working room.
4. **Give the coast broad depth.** The enormous near-planar cliff and capped
   island shapes in `06`–`08` still look like placeholders. A few deliberate
   large ledges, slope changes and a more articulated shoreline will help more
   than surface detail or added distant buildings. Preserve the single-complex
   and single-distant-island structure.
5. **Continue inexpensive form lighting.** Reducing local vertex fill improves
   hotspots, but the broad roof undersides and cavern still read as flat gray
   over large areas. Keep the neutral-white material direction while giving
   these forms readable transitions from several positions. Furniture and
   rooms also remain quite repetitive outside the revised tide arrangement.

The first two items were local completion work for this pass. The main mass and
landscape findings were deliberately retained for the next architectural cycle;
they are not considered resolved by cleaner doors or successful tests.

## Final R05 visual assessment

The critic separately inspected all twenty final views, now preserved in the
[durable R05 snapshot](../../../bird-3d-cursor-projects/Reference/WorldBuildingReviews/20260928-R05),
along with its final route, inventory, editability and passage-clearance records.
All twenty durable PNG hashes match the reviewed local `R05-final` files.
The final local correction addresses the two main first-pass completion issues:

- In `13`, `14`, `18` and `19`, the bright segmented throat joins are no longer
  visible as in the first pass. The passage now reads as one continuous bore.
  The separate tiny cyan slit high in `18` is still visible. Its cause has not
  been established by this visual review, and it remains an open enclosure
  defect rather than an accepted intentional opening.
- In `16`, the tide room now frames mostly open sky and water, with the headland
  at the left edge. The staircase is outside the seated outlook. `17` shows a
  clear entry, and `05` shows the room as a reachable turn off the stair landing.
  The revised L seating gives this destination a distinct social arrangement.
  The widened landing no longer visibly projects across the timber floor in
  the reviewed approach.

### Aesthetics: 6/10

Continuous arrival passages, a simpler cliff portal and the calmer tide-room
outlook improve the architectural coherence. The quiet arrival still reads
forward/left/right, and the gallery's art remains legible. This is a scoped
improvement from R04's 5.5, not a new overall composition.

The main silhouette still consists of two round decks with appended barrel
rooms (`07`). The flat dark underside in `02`/`03` dominates the first exterior
experience, while `20` reads as a circulation platform. `05` exposes broad
shell edges above and behind the lower room; these do not yet read as a unified
occupied cliff-side mass. The giant planar terrain, simple capped landforms
and weak broad lighting remain substantial mismatches with the references.

### Navigability: 8/10 within the reviewed evidence

Forward and side choices are evident at arrival. The smooth side passages and
clean floor transitions are legible in both return and lateral views. The tide
room's approach is now a clear local turn with a visible interior destination,
and its return view faces the guarded landing. All seven final NavMesh paths
remain complete, with 338 sampled standing-capsule checks across the two wing
passages supporting their usable width and floor continuity.

This meets a strong spatial-connectivity and legibility standard for the
reviewed layout. It does not establish comfort for varied avatars, physical
headset use or a newcomer navigating without the saved camera guidance. The
roughly 108 m route to the high lookout also remains a design choice to assess
in actual use. Final revised controller traversal is recorded separately below;
a NavMesh pass alone is not substituted for it.

### Hangout suitability: 6.5/10

The hidden room now offers a calm outlook worth discovering, rather than a
front-row view of stairs or a near cliff. Its L seating and side art focus
differentiate it from the facing-seat booths. Cleaner room thresholds and
reduced local lighting hotspots support a more settled interior experience.

The remaining rooms still repeat similar proportions and furniture, and the
upper cliff lounge continues to frame the staircase strongly in `11`. Large
terraces and the water platform still lack a convincing relationship between
deliberate Bird working space and social edges. Neither the available seats
nor the new view proves comfortable small-group use, voice separation or
seated-avatar suitability.

### Overall VRChat world quality: 6.5/10

R05 improves actual visitor choices and one social destination while retaining
ordinary editable prefabs and a separate architecture scene. That is useful
progress beyond surface decoration. The intended world is still not achieved:
the inhabited flowing massing and coastal setting remain weak, and Bird
onboarding and activities are still reserved by anchors rather than functional
here. Fixed renders and export success cannot provide the missing client,
device and multiplayer experience evidence. The overall 8/10 target has not
been reached.

## Priorities carried into the next cycle

1. **Rework the main occupied form and its support.** Use a restrained
   asymmetrical curved edge or shoulder that visibly joins a substantial
   support, with credible floor thickness and openings. Address exposed shell
   edges seen from the discovery route at the same time. Keep the central
   composition calm, the top lookout sparse and Bird working room generous.
   This is higher impact than more furnishings or detail.
2. **Give the coast broad spatial depth.** Replace the giant flat wall and
   capped-block silhouettes with a few deliberate ledges, slope changes and
   coastline bends. Keep one architectural complex and one distant bounded
   island; retain the tide room's newly achieved open-water framing.
3. **Finish enclosure and broad form lighting.** Trace and close the cyan slit
   visible in `18`; inspect roof/wall junctions while moving. Improve the large
   flat gray undersides without changing the bright neutral-white matte
   material direction. Preserve the now-clean circular passages.
4. **Develop social distinctions and future activity boundaries.** Give the
   cliff lounge a calmer seated outlook where practical and distinguish its
   arrangement. Articulate social edges around the large terraces without
   filling the deliberately empty Bird interaction areas.
5. **Keep evidence proportional to claims.** Retain matching forward/reverse,
   lateral, seated and upper-floor views and rerun routes after geometry moves.
   Later evaluate real client/Quest performance, varied avatars and small
   groups. Headset availability should not block the independent design work.

## Authoring and technical evidence

The inspected R05 source applies a named one-time migration to five existing
prefab assets. It resolves expected objects before mutation, rejects an already
applied revision, keeps ordinary saved meshes and relocates the tide destination
anchor. It does not regenerate the whole scene or replace unrelated children.
The documented migration is scoped, not a general merge or transaction tool.
Future conventional prefab edits remain possible.

The first-pass inventory contains 223 mesh renderers, 30,620 instance triangles,
ten shared materials and 123 colliders. All seven NavMesh destinations are
complete. The editability record reports preserved mesh GUID/reference identity,
scene transforms and authored additions, rejection of invalid dimensions before
mutation, and exact restoration of original vertices. As in R04, the five-mesh
profile updater does not reflow circulation automatically.

The implementer reports that the first Android scene/capture, traversal and SDK
export processes exited zero. The critic read the first-pass records: 338
standing-capsule passage samples passed; fourteen outward/return walk legs
completed in 12,538 frames with a maximum endpoint error of 0.0879 m; the normal
SDK export was 459,261 bytes with 262 GameObjects, 842 components and no
missing/project scripts, with upload-size gates and catalog verification. These
records describe the interim geometry and are superseded for final validation
by the results below.

The final visual snapshot records 223 mesh renderers, 30,028 instance triangles,
ten shared materials and 123 colliders. Its seven NavMesh paths are complete;
the hidden-room path is 63.63 m. Its 338 passage samples use a 0.25 m radius,
1.75 m high capsule over a 3 m by 3 m grid at 0.25 m spacing, with supporting
floor checked. This is sampled geometric clearance rather than varied-avatar
acceptance. The scoped profile preservation record also passes. The implementer
reports process exit zero for this final Android combined check.

## Evidence limits

These are fixed editor renders and standing-capsule geometry checks. They do
not establish physical VR comfort, varied-avatar clearance, seated locomotion,
voice behavior, multiplayer group spacing, stereo depth stability or measured
Quest performance. Renderer/triangle counts are not GPU timings. The
architecture still reserves Bird experiences with anchors; it does not yet
demonstrate functional Bird onboarding or activities in this scene.

The design scores above are final for the frozen R05 visual evidence. The
overall 8/10 target remains unmet, and the listed architectural defects remain
open despite the successful independent walk and export checks.
