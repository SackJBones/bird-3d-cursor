# Coastal R06 independent review

The requested independent critic reviewed the archived concept conversation,
four design references and R06 renders. The target remains at least 8/10 in
aesthetics, navigability, hangout suitability and overall VRChat world quality.
Technical checks do not substitute for these judgments or physical VR testing.

## First pass, rejected for exterior design

Scores: aesthetics **5**, navigability **8**, hangout **6.5**, overall **6**.
The critic accepted the visible pit repair for this stage: views 22–24 and 29
show a continuous stepped bowl, capped seat ends and readable return through
the seating gap. View 18 no longer shows the cyan slit. The arrival and
tide-room outlook remain intact; no new visual route or seating blocker was
found. The pit is still broad and inward-facing, with group comfort unproven.

The first new rock shoulder was a major exterior regression. In views 07/25/26
it appeared as an enormous dark flat wall, dominating the preserved complex
and upper-floor view. View 27 showed narrow strip-like faceting. The proposed
bounded remedy was to lower the coastal crest near cavern-roof scale, rise and
recede inland, and use broad sloping facets to join the ridge. The reviewer
also rejected the old west camera as useful evidence because it was inside
terrain, and requested a pulled-back rear view showing the whole silhouette.

This feedback led to a scoped revision of only the saved rock mesh. The pit,
white rear wall and all existing architecture transforms remained fixed. The
final evidence also reframes rear and west views; those changed cameras are
not direct pixel comparisons with the first pass.

## Revised shoulder

The second pass restores the R05 scores: aesthetics **6/10**, navigability
**8/10**, hangout suitability **6.5/10**, overall **6.5/10**. The reviewer supports
bounded repair acceptance, subject to final walking results and a valid west
capture. The lower/receding shoulder removes the overwhelming screen and now
visibly covers and joins the cavern to the ridge. The pit remains continuous,
the slit remains gone, and interiors/outlooks preserve their composition.

This does not earn a finished-world score increase. The rock still has blunt
vertical base/end planes, an oversized dark cavity and stretched rear facets.
The existing stacked terraces, flat undersides and primitive coastline also
limit the result. Address these through later bounded shaping and lighting
passes without relocating the architecture. The overall 8/10 target is unmet.

Final independent follow-up **accepts the bounded repairs**. The replacement
west view is valid and consistent with the roof/east/rear views: the cavern is
within the ridge with no newly exposed shell. Matching Android/Windows walking
records pass all 34 legs (17 routes), 14,058 route-leg frames plus the completion
call, maximum endpoint error 0.2339 m, and process exit 0. This closes the repair
conditions; scores and remaining design defects are unchanged. The actual Quest
arrival render and limited idle frame observation do not establish physical
comfort or active/multiplayer performance.

## Evidence and limitations

The frozen evidence lives in the heavy repository under
`Reference/WorldBuildingReviews/20260928-R06`. The rejected first-pass exterior
views are retained separately. The technical repair and authoring approach is
documented in `COASTAL-WORLD-R06.md`; exact build/device status is in CHECKPOINT.
The prior R05 scores were **6 / 8 / 6.5 / 6.5**. Older reviews remain intact.

R06 is a repair to editable ordinary prefabs, not completion of the coastal
world. Main inhabited massing, large-scale cliff/shore form, soft white lighting,
plants, sky and the extended curved pond remain broader work. Physical comfort,
multiplayer behavior and device performance require their own evidence.
